  Original: x0_hat = x_t - t * v_pred   (t is per-mol [B], not broadcast)
  Fixed:    x0_hat = x_t - t_atom * v_pred   (t_atom is per-atom [N,1])

            multiplies current (already-scaled) lr → LR compounds each epoch

  Fixed: WARMUP_EPOCHS=5 (10% of training)

QM9 dataset:

  - DFT-optimized 3D coordinates at B3LYP/6-31G(2df,p) level of theory

  - Public domain: Ramakrishnan et al. Scientific Data 2014

  - DFT coordinates are physically stable (electronically optimized)
